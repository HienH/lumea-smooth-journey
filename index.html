Under your skin

How your hair changes

From full hair to smooth, in four stages

BeforeFull hair
Shave + flashGone at the surface
You're hereGrows back finer
After 6Mostly gone
Sent because it's 2 weeks since your first session

Looking worse? That's shedding.

Treated hairs don't vanish at the flash. They die at the root and fall out over the next 1 to 3 weeks, which can look like regrowth. It isn't.

Why the two-week gaps

Only hairs that are actively growing can be switched off, and only some of them grow at any one time. Each session catches the next batch, which is why each regrowth comes back finer and sparser.
